Concepts:
- Why QC is hard?
  - want qubits to interact strongly with one another
  - don't want qubits to interact withe environment
  - want to control or measure them 
- Decoherence 
  - QC leaking information to the environment 
- Quantum Error Correction
- How to scale up QC? 
  - Devices, error correction, systems engineering
- Important applications?
  - looking for *quantumly easy, classically hard, and useful.*
  - Simulate efficiently any physical process that occurs in Nature.
    - e.g., particle collision, molecular chemistry, entangled electrons, superconductor, black hold, early universe.
  
- Hardware (approaches)
  - superconducting qubits
    - Transmons: artificial atoms, carefully fabricated and frequently calibrated
    - microwave resonator 
    - tunabale frequency, tunable couplers etc
  - trapped atoms/ions
    - stable laser -> state preparation for single qubit gates
    - vibration modes -> two qubits gates
  - Neutron Atoms 
    - Atoms are movable
  - silicon spin qubits
  - photonics

- NISQ Era (Noisy Intermediate Scale Quantum)
- FASQ Era (Fault-tolerant Application Scale Quantum)
  - QEC is essential


- Simulating quantum dynamics 
- Overcome noise
  - QEM (mitigation)
    - effectively in current processors, asymptotic overhead cost scales exponentially
  - QEC
- Overhead cost of fault tolerance
  - **Surface code**
