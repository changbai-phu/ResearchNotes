# Research Notes
A collection of my research notes, paper summaries, and technical explorations.
  
This repository is mainly a place to keep track of what I learn while exploring topics in quantum computing areas.


## What is included
- Paper - summaries, key ideas, claims, and my understanding of research papers.
- Concepts - explanations of concepts I come across.
- Implementation -  experiments with coding
- Resources - downloaded papers or books or slides, links to online materials etc. 

  
I hope it can also serve as a record of how my research interests develop over time.
