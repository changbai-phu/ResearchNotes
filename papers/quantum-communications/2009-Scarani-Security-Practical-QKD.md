## Problem - claim
The paper addresses the vulnerability of early physical QKD deployments to practical attacks. The authors assert that robust security proofs must move away from idealized qubit assumptions to incorporate real hardware parameters—such as detector dark counts and multi-photon emissions—without losing the guarantee of unconditional security。
